# Source Examples Reference

Two complete worked examples showing fully populated Sources sections.

## Example 1: AAPL Stock Screen

```
Sources verified as of April 8, 2026.

Official Filings & Disclosures
  Apple Inc. — Form 10-Q (Q1 FY2026) — Filed Jan 31, 2026 — [SEC EDGAR]
  Apple Inc. — Q1 FY2026 Earnings Press Release — Jan 30, 2026 — [Apple IR]

Company Pages
  Apple Inc. — Investor Relations: Stock Information — Accessed Apr 8, 2026 — [Link]

Financial Data Providers
  Yahoo Finance — AAPL quote and historical data — Accessed Apr 8, 2026 — [Link]
  NASDAQ — AAPL listing information — Accessed Apr 8, 2026 — [Link]

Financial Publications & News
  Bloomberg — "Apple Revenue Beats Estimates on iPhone Demand" — Apr 1, 2026 — [Link]
  Reuters — "Apple Sees Strong Services Growth in Q2 Guidance" — Jan 31, 2026 — [Link]
  Wall Street Journal — "FTC Expands Probe Into App Store Practices" — Mar 22, 2026 — [Link]
  Barron's — "Goldman Sachs Upgrades Apple to Buy" — Apr 5, 2026 — [Link]
  CNBC — "Apple Announces $100B Buyback Program" — Feb 15, 2026 — [Link]
  MarketWatch — "Apple Services Revenue Hits New Record" — Jan 31, 2026 — [Link]
```

### Notes on This Example

- SEC filing is listed first (highest tier).
- Company IR page is separate from filings.
- Yahoo Finance is the data provider for price and volume history.
- NASDAQ provides listing-specific information.
- News articles are listed chronologically within their group.
- Each entry has: source, title/description, date, and link placeholder.
- No source is listed twice even though Yahoo Finance was used for multiple
  data points throughout the dashboard.

## Example 2: SPY ETF Screen

```
Sources verified as of April 8, 2026.

Fund Sponsor
  State Street Global Advisors — SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust Product Page — Accessed Apr 8, 2026 — [Link]
  State Street Global Advisors — SPY Daily Holdings — Apr 8, 2026 — [Link]
  State Street Global Advisors — SPY Fact Sheet — Q1 2026 — [Link]

Official Filings & Disclosures
  SPY — Semi-Annual Report (N-CSRS) — Filed Mar 15, 2026 — [SEC EDGAR]

Financial Data Providers
  Yahoo Finance — SPY quote and historical data — Accessed Apr 8, 2026 — [Link]
  ETF.com — SPY fund flows and analytics — Accessed Apr 8, 2026 — [Link]
  ETFdb.com — SPY expense ratio comparison — Accessed Apr 8, 2026 — [Link]

Financial Publications & News
  Bloomberg — "S&P 500 ETFs See Record Inflows in Q1 2026" — Apr 3, 2026 — [Link]
  Reuters — "US Equity ETFs Attract $50B in March" — Apr 1, 2026 — [Link]
  Wall Street Journal — "Passive Investing Passes 50% of US Fund Assets" — Mar 18, 2026 — [Link]
```

### Notes on This Example

- Fund sponsor (SSGA) is listed first and gets three entries because
  the product page, daily holdings, and fact sheet are distinct resources.
- ETF-specific data providers (ETF.com, ETFdb.com) appear alongside
  general providers.
- News articles for ETFs are often about the asset class or index rather
  than the specific fund — this is expected and appropriate.

## Format Rules Demonstrated

1. Group headings are bold and match the prescribed order.
2. Each entry follows: [Source] — "Title/Description" — Date — [Link].
3. Dates use "Accessed" for web pages and specific dates for publications.
4. No source appears in multiple groups.
5. The section is compact — no excessive whitespace or decoration.
