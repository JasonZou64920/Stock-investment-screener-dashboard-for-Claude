# Source Citation Standards

How to attribute factual claims and organize sources across screening dashboards.

## Principles

1. Every non-trivial data point has a source. Exceptions: derived calculations, universally known facts.
2. Prefer primary over secondary (10-K > MarketWatch summary).
3. Links must resolve. Never fabricate URLs.
4. Transparency: if a lower-tier source was used, say so.

## Source Hierarchy

- **Tier 1 (Primary):** SEC filings (EDGAR), company IR, ETF sponsor pages, exchange data, government sources.
- **Tier 2 (Established):** Yahoo Finance, Google Finance, Bloomberg, Reuters, WSJ, FT, Barron's, ETF.com, ETFdb.com.
- **Tier 3 (Secondary):** CNBC, MarketWatch, Seeking Alpha, Morningstar, Finviz, trade press.
- **Not acceptable as sole source:** Forums, social media, content marketing, no-author articles, Wikipedia for financial data.

## Inline Citations

Pattern: factual claim (Source Name, Date).
- "Revenue grew 12% YoY (Apple 10-Q, Jan 2026)."
- "Expense ratio is 0.03% (Vanguard.com, accessed Apr 2026)."
- Shortest recognizable name. Include date (min month/year). "Accessed [date]" for web data.
- No numeric footnotes — name-based citations scan faster.

## Section-Level Attribution

For metric strips and tables: caption at section bottom in 11px whisper style.
"Data: Yahoo Finance, Vanguard.com | As of Apr 8, 2026"

## Direct Links

- Clickable descriptive text, not raw URLs. Style: accent color, underline on hover.
- Link to specific pages, not homepages.
- Never fabricate. Cite by name without link if uncertain.

## Consolidated Sources Section

Grouped in order: Official Filings & Disclosures, Fund Sponsor / Company Pages, Financial Data Providers, Financial Publications & News, Other.

Entry format: [Source] — "Title" — Date — [Link]

Each source listed once (no duplicates). Caption: "Sources verified as of [date]."

## Special Cases

- **Conflicting sources**: use higher tier, note material discrepancies (>10%).
- **Paywalled**: still cite, include URL, don't note the paywall.
- **Derived calculations**: only source data needs attribution.
- **Timing**: master timestamp in header, "Sources verified" in sources section.
